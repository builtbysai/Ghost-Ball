How to play

  1. Aim. Move on the cloth to swing the cue around the cue ball. Drag the FINE AIM edge for thin cuts.
  2. Pull. Press and draw back. The cue follows your hand and the aim locks.
  3. Push. Push forward to strike: the speed of your push is the speed of the shot. Or let go, and the shot fires from how far you drew.

Tap the cue-ball button to move the strike point: high follows, low draws, sides bend the rebound. Double-tap centers it.

After your group is cleared, call the pocket for the 8 ball by tapping it. A foul hands your opponent the cue ball.

Keys: arrows fine-aim, Space pulls and pushes, Esc cancels a pull or pauses. A gamepad works too: left stick aims, right stick is the cue.
