## Rookie / planner root causes

Measured with `scripts/full-rack-lab.mjs` over seeded Rookie-vs-Rookie racks.

1. **Candidate pots were almost never makeable** (0 of 120 noise-free candidates potted on post-break tables). Two causes: shots needing a cut of 90° or more (the cue ball moves *away* from the contact point and misses entirely), and straight lines to a corner-pocket centre that run through the rubber jaw nose. `candidateShots` now rejects cuts past ~78°, aims across the pocket mouth at the first jaw-clear line, and prices off-centre aims.
2. **Rookie never looked ahead.** It now vets up to 4 (8 when stalled) candidates in real physics, takes the first makeable one, and re-draws its aim blur (bounded) if the blurred stroke would itself foul. It still misses pots like a casual player, but not rules.
3. **No real fallback.** With no pot available it played a straight low-power "contact" shot, which produced most no-rail / no-contact fouls. Both tiers now share `safetyPlan`: legal first contact with a rail afterwards, powers raised and thin contacts tried around blockers. Club Pro keeps its original option grid.
4. **No stall breaker.** `Game.dryTurns` counts a seat's consecutive turns without a pot and widens Rookie's search and tightens its blur after two.

| Rookie vs Rookie, 12-16 seeds | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Racks finished | 12/12 | 16/16 |
| Mean shots per rack | 106 | 80 |
| Foul rate | 24% | 2% |
| no-rail / no-contact fouls | 125 / 88 | 1 / 2 |

Planning is frame-sliced for every non-break CPU shot (previously only Club Pro), so the extra look-ahead never blocks a frame.

## Physics

A ball moving under 70 units/s within 9 units outside a pocket well now drops in. Previously it could balance on the lip indefinitely (play-test finding, "ball balanced on a pocket lip for many turns").

## Not changed

Referee, group assignment, ball-in-hand routing and shot-clock expiry were re-verified correct (see findings section 6A); the confusion they caused was presentational and is addressed by P4.
